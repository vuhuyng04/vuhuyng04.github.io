I am a data scientist and AI engineer working on natural language processing, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic AI systems. My work spans the full path from data to a deployed model: exploration and feature engineering, training and evaluation, and serving the result behind an API.

I am currently in the **advanced track (Levels 3–4)** of [VinUni × Vingroup's AI in Action (AI20k)](https://vinuni.edu.vn/) programme, Cohort IV — an intensive, SFIA-aligned programme pairing coursework with applied AI projects delivered at Vingroup companies. There I build production-grade agentic systems: multi-agent orchestration with LangGraph, tool integration over the Model Context Protocol and agent-to-agent communication, retrieval-augmented generation, and LLM evaluation and guardrails, with LLMOps and observability practices to keep them reliable and cost-aware.

Before this I was a Data Science Collaborator at **TPBank**, applying CRISP-DM to banking data, and an on-the-job training intern at **FPT Software**, where I built a retrieval-augmented generation chatbot with Python, Qdrant, and FastAPI.

I hold a **Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence** from [FPT University](https://fpt.edu.vn/), where my thesis built an intelligent CV–job description matching model for recruitment decision support. Through [AI VIET NAM](https://aivietnam.edu.vn/)'s one-year AIO2024 residency and its research group, I have published peer-reviewed work on **low-resource machine translation** (FETC 2025, first author) and **object detection in adverse weather** (CSoNet 2025).
