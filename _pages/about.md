I am a data scientist and AI engineer working on natural language processing, retrieval-augmented generation, and applied machine learning. My work spans the full path from data to a deployed model: exploration and feature engineering, training and evaluation, and serving the result behind an API.

I hold a **B.S. in Artificial Intelligence** from [FPT University](https://fpt.edu.vn/), and completed [AI VIET NAM](https://aivietnam.edu.vn/)'s one-year AIO2024 residency — 300+ sessions across mathematics for AI, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, NLP, and generative AI. Through its research group I have published peer-reviewed work on **low-resource machine translation** (FETC 2025, first author) and **object detection in adverse weather** (CSoNet 2025).

Most recently I worked as a Data Science Collaborator at **TPBank**, applying CRISP-DM to banking data, and before that as an on-the-job training intern at **FPT Software**, where I built a retrieval-augmented generation chatbot with Python, Qdrant, and FastAPI.
